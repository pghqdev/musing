## Chrome Web Store Description

**Short description (132 chars):**
> Personalized quotes on every new tab, matched to your AI conversations. Fully private - all processing happens locally.

**Detailed description:**
> Transform your new tab into a moment of reflection. Musing analyzes your conversations with Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini to surface relevant quotes from history's greatest thinkers.
>
> **100% Private**: All processing happens on your device. No data ever leaves your browser. No accounts, no tracking, no servers.
>
> **How it works**:
> • Reads conversation themes from your AI chats
> • Matches themes to a curated database of 200+ quotes
> • Displays relevant wisdom on every new tab
>
> **Features**:
> • Beautiful, minimal new tab with inspirational quotes
> • Multiple search engines (Google, DuckDuckGo, Wolfram Alpha, GitHub)
> • Works with Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini
> • Zero data collection - everything stays on your device
