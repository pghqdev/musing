Privacy Policy

What We Collect

We extract themes from your AI conversations (Claude.ai, ChatGPT) to find relevant historical quotes. Only extracted themes are sent to our server, not full conversations.

How It's Used

Themes are used solely to query our quote database and return relevant quotes. We do not use your data for training, advertising, or any other purpose.

Data Storage

Conversation snippets are stored locally on your device. Theme data sent to our server is not permanently stored and is discarded after processing.

Your Rights

You can disable data collection at any time using the toggles above. To delete all local data, remove the extension from Chrome.
